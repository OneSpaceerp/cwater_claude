Monitor · Monitoring & Reporting

A Record You Can Act On.

Continuous measurement turned into periodic reporting that says what changed and what to do.

Discuss Monitoring & ReportingTalk to an Engineer

01What It Is

The Service.

Capture of treatment and system data, review against the programme’s control limits, and periodic reporting that turns the record into decisions.

02Why It Matters

Why It Matters.

Data that nobody reviews is storage, not monitoring. The value is in someone technical looking at the trend and saying what it means.

03When You Need It

When to Ask for It.

  • Evidence is required

    Audit, compliance or internal reporting that needs a documented record of condition.

  • Several systems or sites

    Consistent reporting is what makes different systems comparable.

  • You want to improve something

    Without a baseline record, an improvement cannot be demonstrated.

04The C-Water Approach

How It Is Carried Out.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Controller data, test results and consumption records brought into one place.

  2. 02

    Review against limits

    Time within control limits is a more useful measure than an average.

  3. 03

    Trend

    Across enough time to separate a real change from normal variation.

  4. 04

    Report with a recommendation

    Every report ends with what should happen next, or explicitly that nothing needs to.

05What You Receive

The Output.

  • Periodic technical report on system condition
  • Trend analysis against control limits
  • Consumption and performance summary
  • Recommended actions, prioritised
