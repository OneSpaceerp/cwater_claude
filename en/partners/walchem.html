01Capability areas

What This Technology Contributes.

01

Water treatment controllers

Online analytical controllers that read process conditions, act on setpoints and log what happened. The Intuition and W100 families cover cooling tower, boiler and general water-treatment duties.

02

Sensors and measurement

pH and ORP electrodes, contacting and electrodeless conductivity, disinfection sensors, turbidity, dissolved oxygen, corrosion rate and fluorometric measurement.

03

Electronic metering pumps

Solenoid and motor-driven metering pumps across the IX, E-Series and LK families, including high-viscosity duties, sized to the chemical programme rather than to a standard catalogue line.

04

Connected water management

Walchem Fluent is a cloud platform for remote visibility of controller data — trends, alarms and treatment status made available away from the plant room.
