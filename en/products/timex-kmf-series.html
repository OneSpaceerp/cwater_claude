01Overview

What It Is.

A self-cleaning filter earns its cost where manual element cleaning would otherwise be frequent. C-Water sizes to the measured solids load, its nature and the available differential pressure — not to nominal pipe diameter.

02Key Benefits

Why It Is Specified.

01

Cleans without stopping flow

Suited to systems that cannot be taken offline to service a filter element.

02

Removes a manual task

Where manual cleaning is frequent, automation is usually justified on labour alone.

03

Cleaning frequency as data

How often the filter cleans is a direct readout of what is arriving in the water.

03Applications

Where It Is Used.

  • Cooling tower side-stream filtration
  • Irrigation network protection
  • Process and produced water
  • RO pretreatment
